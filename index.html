Behind the Balaklava

ALIK emerged as an artist in 2020 within the Bologna rave scene. The following year, he transitioned to clubs — performing in Bologna, Florence, Parma, Modena, La Spezia, and Milan — while bringing the same underground rave spirit to the DJ booth.

His sound spans Industrial, Hardtechno, Hardtek, and Uptempo.

Also in 2021, he co-founded Balaklava Club with friends — a collective that goes beyond music by bringing art and live performances into the club environment.
